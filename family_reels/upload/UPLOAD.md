# Instagram'ga yuklash

Fayllar: `../final/family_happiness_reels.mp4`, `cover.jpg`, `caption.txt`

## Moslik (tekshirilgan)
1080x1920 (9:16), 30 FPS, H.264 yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz stereo, 42 s (Reels limiti 90 s dan kam), 21 MB, `moov` boshida (faststart), ≈ -16 LUFS.

## Qadamlar (telefon ilovasi yoki Meta Business Suite)
1. Videoni ASL fayl sifatida yuklang (Telegram/WhatsApp orqali yuborilgan siqilgan nusxadan emas).
2. **Cover** → "Galereyadan" → `cover.jpg`. Profil setkasida 3:4 kesilganda ham matn ko‘rinadi.
3. Caption: `caption.txt` ni qo‘ying (hashtaglar 10–12 ta).
4. Instagram'ning avtomatik subtitr/stiker qo‘shishini o‘chiring (videoda subtitr bor).
5. Pastki ~250 px va o‘ng chetdagi tugmalar zonasi subtitr bilan to‘qnashmaydi (tekshirilgan).
6. Musiqa qo‘shmang (ovoz -16 LUFS, Instagram musiqasi diktorni bosib ketadi).
7. Ulashishdan oldin ilovada bir marta to‘liq ko‘rib, ovozni eshiting.
